TOPIC: Atomic Structure
QUESTION: Which subatomic particle determines the atomic number of an element?
TYPE: multiple-choice
OPTIONS: Proton|Neutron|Electron|Photon
ANSWER: Proton
EXPLANATION: The atomic number is defined as the number of protons in the nucleus.

TOPIC: Atomic Structure
QUESTION: Isotopes of the same element have the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons.
TYPE: true-false
OPTIONS: True|False
ANSWER: True
EXPLANATION: Isotopes are atoms of one element with different neutron counts.

TOPIC: Chemical Bonding
QUESTION: Which bond forms by the transfer of electrons from one atom to another?
TYPE: multiple-choice
OPTIONS: Covalent bond|Hydrogen bond|Ionic bond|Metallic bond
ANSWER: Ionic bond
EXPLANATION: Ionic bonds form when electrons are transferred, creating oppositely charged ions.

TOPIC: Chemical Bonding
QUESTION: A molecule of O2 contains a double covalent bond.
TYPE: true-false
OPTIONS: True|False
ANSWER: True
EXPLANATION: Each oxygen atom shares two electrons, giving a double bond.

TOPIC: Stoichiometry
QUESTION: What is the molar mass of H2O?
TYPE: multiple-choice
OPTIONS: 16 g/mol|18 g/mol|20 g/mol|22 g/mol
ANSWER: 18 g/mol
EXPLANATION: H2O has two hydrogens (2 x 1) and one oxygen (16), totaling 18 g/mol.

QUESTION: Which process is endothermic?
TYPE: multiple-choice
OPTIONS: Freezing water|Condensing steam|Evaporation of water|Combustion of methane
ANSWER: Evaporation of water

TOPIC: Equilibrium
QUESTION: Increasing pressure favors the side with fewer gas moles in a gaseous equilibrium.
TYPE: true-false
OPTIONS: True|False
ANSWER: True
EXPLANATION: By Le Chatelier's principle, pressure increase shifts equilibrium to reduce gas moles.
